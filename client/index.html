1. Open this page on your Computer and click Start Streaming.

3. Scan the QR code with your Phone to start listening instantly.

About

Audio Streamer lets you stream system audio from one device to another in real time using WebRTC. It’s perfect for watching movies, listening to music, or working in environments where installing software is not possible — all directly from your browser.
